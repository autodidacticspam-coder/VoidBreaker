# VOIDBREAKER
## Project Overview

**Genre:** Space Roguelike / Real-Time Tactical Strategy
**Engine:** Unity 2022 LTS (C#)
**Target Platform:** PC (Steam), with future console potential
**Development Codename:** Project Chrysalis

---

## THE VIBE

*"Your ship is alive. It remembers every battle, every sacrifice, every desperate choice. As you flee across the dying sectors of a collapsing galaxy, your vessel doesn't just carry upgrades—it EVOLVES. Weapons fuse with hull. Crew quarters grow organic. The line between ship and crew blurs. By the time you reach your destination, you won't recognize what you've become... but neither will your enemies."*

---

## ELEVATOR PITCH

**VoidBreaker** is the spiritual successor to FTL that asks: *"What if your ship was a living thing that grew and mutated based on how you played?"*

Flee through 8 sectors of a dying galaxy while being pursued by the Hegemony fleet. Make desperate choices, manage your crew, and watch your ship PHYSICALLY TRANSFORM based on your decisions. A stealth player's ship grows dark and angular. A combat player's ship bristles with bio-mechanical weapons. A diplomat's ship develops communication arrays that look almost... friendly.

Three unique final confrontations await based on your evolution path—there is no "wrong build."

---

## CORE PILLARS

### 1. THE LIVING SHIP
Your ship visually and mechanically evolves. Not just stats—actual visual transformation. Mutation points accumulate from combat, events, and crew actions. Spend them on three evolution paths:
- **PREDATOR** (Combat) - Weapons fuse with hull, aggressive appearance
- **PHANTOM** (Stealth) - Ship becomes darker, more angular, harder to detect
- **HERALD** (Diplomacy) - Communication arrays, inviting design, crew bonuses

### 2. MEANINGFUL CHOICE, NOT SLOT MACHINE
- **Drafting:** Choose 1 of 3 rewards after each encounter
- **Pity Timers:** Guaranteed weapon/crew by sector 3 if RNG is cruel
- **Meta Progression:** Unlock "Gene Memories" that provide starting bonuses
- **Telegraphed Danger:** See threat level before jumping

### 3. CREW AS FAMILY
Crew members develop relationships, conflicts, and bonds over a run. A crew that's been through hell together fights harder. Lose a beloved crewmate and morale craters. Optional permadeath for hardcore players.

### 4. THREE DESTINIES
Your evolution path determines your final boss:
- **PREDATOR** → Face the Hegemony Dreadnought in brutal ship combat
- **PHANTOM** → Infiltrate the Hegemony Command Station
- **HERALD** → Negotiate/manipulate the Hegemony Council
